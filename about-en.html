About us: RAX3D 3D Printing in Saudi Arabia

RAX3D is a Saudi shop specialised in 3D printing. We turn designs into practical, good-looking pieces for the home, the desk and gifts, and print each one to order in the size and colour you choose.

What we offer

  • Home and office decor: 3D-printed vases and decorative pieces.
  • Distinctive gifts and cups, printed to order.
  • Key chains and whistles in a variety of designs.
  • xBloom coffee accessories.
  • Orders for shops and giveaways in bulk, priced by quantity.

How it works

  • 3D preview: view and rotate the piece from every angle before you order.
  • 13 colours: pick the colour, size and quantity, and we print it for you.
  • Flexible payment: online by card or Apple Pay, cash on delivery, or send the order by message.
  • Clear lead time: 3 to 7 days for regular orders, and you can track your order by its number.
  • Loyalty points: earn one point for every riyal and redeem points for a discount or free delivery.

For shops and giveaways

We accept bulk orders for shops, events and giveaways. Price and lead time depend on the order and the quantity, and we contact you by message to agree the details.
