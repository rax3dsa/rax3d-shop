About us

RAX3D is a Saudi 3D printing shop. We turn designs into practical, good-looking pieces, printed to order in the colours and sizes you choose.

What we offer

  • Home and office decor: vases and decorative pieces.
  • Distinctive gifts and cups.
  • Key chains and whistles.
  • xBloom coffee accessories.

How it works

  • Pick the piece, colour, size and quantity.
  • Preview it in 3D before you order.
  • We print it after your order is confirmed and prepare it within 3 to 7 days.
  • Receive it by delivery, or arrange a direct pickup with us.

For shops and giveaways

We take orders by the piece and in bulk. The wholesale price applies automatically from 12 pieces, or from 6 pieces for items weighing 100 g or more. Lead time for wholesale orders is agreed with you based on quantity.

Loyalty points

You earn one point for every riyal you pay, and you can redeem points for a discount or a free piece.

Have an idea or a custom order? Message us on Instagram @rax3d.sa, or browse the pieces and order straight from the shop.
